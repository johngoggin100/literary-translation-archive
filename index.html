About

What this is

The Literary Translation Archive sets different English translations of the same passages side by side, so the choices a translator makes — in register, diction, and meaning — are visible at a glance. It is a reading and teaching tool, not a publisher of full texts.

Copyright

Translations published before 1930, and other works whose copyright has expired, are in the public domain. Later translations are protected by copyright; this archive shows only short excerpts of those works for the purpose of noncommercial commentary, comparison, and scholarship — a use generally permitted by fair use (US) and fair dealing (UK/EU). No full-length texts are reproduced, and excerpts are kept as short as possible.

Rights holders

If you are a translator or publisher and would like a passage removed or a credit corrected, please open an issue on the project repository or contact the maintainer there. We will respond promptly.

Sources

The catalogue is informed by Boyd Tonkin, The 100 Best Novels in Translation (2018). Each comparison column notes the translator, the year of publication, and, where known, the publisher.

Third-party data

Book covers and work descriptions come from Open Library, whose data is public domain (CC0). Author portraits and biographical summaries come from Wikipedia and are used under the CC BY-SA 4.0 license.
